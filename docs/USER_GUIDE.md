# WasteLoop — User Guide (Citizen & Collector Portals)

---

## Part 1: Citizen Eco Portal User Guide

### 1.1 Zero-Effort Philosophy
As a citizen, you do **NOT** need to weigh your garbage every day or manually record routine waste generation. 
You set your automated recurring schedule once, and standard waste pickups happen automatically.

### 1.2 Setting Up Your Recurring Collection Schedule
1. Log into WasteLoop using Email/Password or **"Continue with Google"**.
2. Click **"Configure Recurring Schedule"** on your dashboard to open the 5-step wizard:
   - **Step 1 (Location)**: Confirm your pickup address. Click **"GPS Detect"** to pin your exact coordinates (`16.4344° N, 81.6965° E`).
   - **Step 2 (Waste Types)**: Select waste categories generated by your household (Wet Organic, Dry Recyclable, Hazardous/E-Waste, Glass & Metal).
   - **Step 3 (Frequency)**: Choose collection frequency (Daily, Every 2 Days, Every 3 Days, Twice a Week, Weekly).
   - **Step 4 (Time Slot)**: Select your preferred time window (Morning 8-10 AM, Afternoon 1-3 PM, Evening 5-7 PM).
   - **Step 5 (Review & Activate)**: Review summary details and click **"Activate Collection Schedule"**.

### 1.3 Managing Your Schedule
- Navigate to the **"My Schedule"** tab to view your weekly pickup matrix.
- Click **"Pause Service"** when traveling to stop upcoming pickups. Click **"Resume Schedule"** upon returning.
- Click **"Edit Schedule"** at any time to adjust frequency, time windows, or address.

### 1.4 Reporting Issues with Camera Evidence
1. Click **"Report Issue"** on your dashboard.
2. Select the issue category (Missed Collection, Bin Overflow, Illegal Dumping, Damaged Bin).
3. Click **"Take Photo"** to open the live Camera viewfinder or **"Upload Image"** to select a photo from your device.
4. Review the auto-generated Evidence ID (`WL-EV-2026-XXXXXX`) and AI verification score.
5. Click **"Submit Complaint Ticket"** to dispatch the ticket directly to your Zone Supervisor.

### 1.5 Special Waste Concierge
For bulky furniture (sofas, tables), mattresses, appliances (refrigerators, washing machines), or e-waste:
1. Click **"Special Waste"** tab.
2. Select your item card (e.g. Refrigerator 🧊 or Furniture 🛋️).
3. Confirm preferred pickup date, time, and address.
4. Click **"Confirm Special Pickup Request"**.
5. View the **Special Waste Collection Confirmed** screen and click **"View Collection"** to track dispatch.

### 1.6 AI Waste Disposal Guide
1. Click **"AI Waste Guide"** tab.
2. Search any item (e.g. "batteries", "pizza box", "plastic bottle") or click **"Take Photo"**.
3. View instant AI classification, confidence score, and recommended disposal instructions.

## Part 2: Field Collector Mobile Guide

### 2.1 Starting Your Shift & Route
1. Log in with your Collector account credentials or select the **Field Collector** demo role.
2. View your header stats: Today's Route, Stops Remaining, Completed Stops, and Vehicle Load.
3. Check your **GPS Accuracy** indicator (`GPS Accuracy: ±8m`). Click **"Update GPS"** if needed.

### 2.2 Servicing Collection Stops
1. Select your active stop from the stop list.
2. Click **`[ Start Navigation ]`** to view road routing via OpenStreetMap / OSRM.
3. Click **`[ Arrived ]`** upon reaching the pickup location.
4. Click **`[ Mark Collected ]`**, enter the verified pickup weight in kg (e.g. `8.5 kg`), and check segregation status.
5. Click **"Confirm Collection"** to record pickup and advance to the next stop.

### 2.3 Handling Exceptions & Offline Mode
- **Skip Stop**: If a location is unreachable, click **`[ Skip ]`** to proceed to the next stop.
- **Report Issue**: Click **`[ Report Issue ]`** to alert your supervisor of road blockages or damaged bins.
- **Offline Mode**: If network connectivity drops, the header shows **`Offline (Sync Queue)`**. All collection actions save locally to your device and sync automatically upon reconnection.
